Visit & Events

I’m Jake. Most everything in the gallery I found in thrift stores around San Antonio — I drive a lot, and I stop in wherever I can. That habit has turned up a lot of treasures, and the gallery is as much a record of the collection as it is a place to find your next piece.

Come hang out — there’s art, wine, and friends, and no pressure to do anything but enjoy it.

Where

Behind Contemporary / Bar 1919 in Southtown. Visits are by appointment for now; address on request — email jake@rescued.art.

By appointment

Email jake@rescued.art and we’ll find a time that works.
